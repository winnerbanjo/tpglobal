About TP Global Services

The Story & Brand Philosophy

Built on African excellence, international mobility, and bespoke luxury service across Nigeria and Cape Verde.

Founder & Creative Director

A Vision of Seamless Elegance

TP Global Services was founded with a singular ambition: to provide clients with one trusted, high-calibre partner for lifestyle, bespoke fashion, overseas journey coordination, celebrations, and personal pampering.

Our founder, known simply as TP, recognized that modern professionals and diaspora families were tired of fragmented service providers and endless forwarded pictures over messaging apps. TP created a unified standard:

01

Authentic African Craftsmanship

Celebrated fabrics including Italian cottons, luxury silks, rich linens, and handwoven Aso-Oke tailored to perfection.

02

Transnational Footprint

Physical contact points and on-the-ground support in Lekki, Lagos (Nigeria) and Praça Center Palmarejo (Cape Verde).

03

Bespoke Attention to Every Client

Direct communication, transparent pricing, and respectful, personalized care from initial enquiry to final delivery.
