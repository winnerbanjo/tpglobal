Cape Verde Archipelago Tours

Featured Cape Verde Island Explorations

Explore Cape Verde's most coveted Atlantic islands with on-the-ground support, VIP airport pickups, and curated excursions.

Most Popular Resort Island
✈️ Amílcar Cabral International Airport (Sal International Airport)

Sal Island (Ilha do Sal)

Top Tourist Sites & Excursions:
  • Santa Maria Beach & Pier: World-renowned golden sand beach, crystal turquoise waters, and vibrant beachfront dining.
  • Shark Bay (Baía da Parda): Safe, thrilling wading with wild lemon sharks in their crystal shallows.
  • Buracona & The Blue Eye (Olho Azul): Breathtaking volcanic natural pool illuminated by the mid-day sun.
  • Pedra de Lume Salt Crater: Buoyant floating mineral spa nestled inside an extinct volcanic caldera.
  • Kite Beach: World-class kitesurfing and ocean breeze promenades.
Dunes & Coastal Serenity
✈️ Aristides Pereira International Airport

Boa Vista Island

Top Tourist Sites & Excursions:
  • Praia de Chaves: Dramatic shifting desert dunes meeting the Atlantic ocean.
  • Deserto de Viana: White Sahara sand desert formed by ocean trade winds; quad safari favorite.
  • Santa Monica Beach: 18 kilometers of untouched, breathtaking golden coastal paradise.
  • Shipwreck of Cabo Santa Maria: Famous 1968 Spanish cargo ship scenic coastal landmark.
  • Morro de Areia Nature Reserve: Protected loggerhead sea turtle nesting sanctuaries.
Culture & Commercial Hub
✈️ Nelson Mandela International Airport (Praia)

Santiago Island (Praia Hub)

Top Tourist Sites & Excursions:
  • Cidade Velha: UNESCO World Heritage Site, 16th-century Forte Real de São Filipe, and historic Pelourinho.
  • Tarrafal Beach: Iconic coconut palm bay sheltered by lush mountain peaks.
  • Serra Malagueta Natural Park: Volcanic mountain ridges, rich eco-trails, and panoramic valleys.
  • Sucupira Market & Palmarejo: Bustling capital culture, artisan crafts, and TP Global Services direct hub.

Founder & Creative Director

A Vision of Seamless Elegance

TP Global Services was founded with a singular ambition: to provide clients with one trusted, high-calibre partner for lifestyle, bespoke fashion, overseas journey coordination, celebrations, and personal pampering.

Our founder, known simply as TP, recognized that modern professionals and diaspora families were tired of fragmented service providers and endless forwarded pictures over messaging apps. TP created a unified standard:

01

Authentic African Craftsmanship

Celebrated fabrics including Italian cottons, luxury silks, rich linens, and handwoven Aso-Oke tailored to perfection.

02

Transnational Footprint

Physical contact points and on-the-ground support in Lekki, Lagos (Nigeria) and Praça Center Palmarejo (Cape Verde).

03

Bespoke Attention to Every Client

Direct communication, transparent pricing, and respectful, personalized care from initial enquiry to final delivery.
